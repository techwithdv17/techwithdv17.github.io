How it works.
  1. The page loads the tools.
    The first separation downloads a 136 MB model and a browser runtime. Internet is needed to load them.
  2. Your browser separates the audio.
    Your song stays on this computer. We process it in short sections and join the results into aligned tracks.
  3. You take the stems with you.
    Listen here, then download individual WAV files or all six in a ZIP. Saved exports stay yours when the tab closes.
Early speed check: On an M1 Mac with 8 GB RAM in Chrome, 8-second tests took between 43 seconds and 3 minutes including setup; a 30-second test took 7 minutes 41 seconds. Timings vary with system load. Full songs may take a long time. Try the short demo before choosing your own file.
Browser compatibility: Start with a current desktop Chrome or Edge. Mac and Windows share the same web app; speed and memory limits vary by device. Other browsers need further testing.
The model is downloaded only when you separate a song.
