A straightforward workflow

From song to stems.

01 / CHOOSE

Bring your song.

Select an MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC, or another supported audio file. Or use a supported single-song link, including YouTube and SoundCloud.

02 / CONFIRM

Listen before you start.

Hear the first 30 seconds or less, check the title, and confirm the song. A different song needs its own confirmation.

03 / SEPARATE

Save all six tracks.

Watch progress, cancel if needed, and open your finished WAVs. Add a ZIP if you want the export packaged together.

Import all six WAV files at time zero in GarageBand or another editor. Their relative levels are preserved with a shared gain adjustment when needed.

Good to know

Before you try it.

Does this website process my music?

No. This site introduces the desktop app and its releases. Local-file preview and stem separation happen in the installed app on your Mac. Downloading a song link connects to the supported media site.

Can I use song links?

The app can download a supported public single-song link, then lets you preview and confirm it. Use audio you have permission to download. Playlists, live streams, private or protected media are not supported, and site availability can change.

How much space does it need?

The current application occupies roughly 676 MB on the tested Mac. Songs, temporary processing files, WAV stems, and optional ZIPs need additional space. The app checks available storage before separation.

Is there a mixer or formation editor?

Those are planned for later releases. The current app exports stems that you can open in GarageBand or another audio editor.

What powers the separation?

The desktop application uses the pretrained Demucs htdemucs_6s model. It estimates vocals, drums, bass, guitar, piano, and other accompaniment; it cannot isolate every instrument individually.

Powered by Demucs. The desktop interface and processing workflow are part of this project.
