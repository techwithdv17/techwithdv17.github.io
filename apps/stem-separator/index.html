A straightforward workflow

From song to stems.

01 / CHOOSE

Bring your song.

Choose an MP3, WAV, M4A, or other browser-supported audio file. The web beta uses local files. Song-link downloading is available only in the desktop evaluation.

02 / CONFIRM

Listen before you start.

Listen to the audio, check the title, and confirm the song. Choose a different file and the app will ask you to confirm it again.

03 / SEPARATE

Save all six tracks.

Watch progress, cancel if needed, and open your finished WAVs. Add a ZIP if you want the export packaged together.

Explore the outputs

Import all six WAV files at time zero in GarageBand or another editor. Their relative levels are preserved with a shared gain adjustment when needed.

Good to know

Before you try it.

Where is my music processed?

The web beta processes audio locally in your browser. The model and runtime download from public hosts, but your song is not uploaded. The desktop evaluation also processes local files on your computer.

Can I use song links?

The web beta currently accepts local audio files only. The desktop evaluation can download supported public single-song links for audio you have permission to download. Playlists, live streams, private and protected media are not supported.

How much space does it need?

The browser downloads a 136 MB model plus its runtime and can keep the model cached for later use. Separation also needs available memory, and downloaded WAVs and ZIPs need storage. The desktop evaluation occupies roughly 676 MB on the tested Mac.

Is there a mixer or formation editor?

Those are planned for later releases. The current app exports stems that you can open in GarageBand or another audio editor.

What powers the separation?

The desktop application uses the pretrained Demucs htdemucs_6s model. It estimates vocals, drums, bass, guitar, piano, and other accompaniment; it cannot isolate every instrument individually.
